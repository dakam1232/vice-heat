VICE HEAT is an original open-world crime game that runs entirely in a desktop browser. Explore a procedurally generated coastal city at sunset, steal cars, outrun the police through the street grid, scatter pedestrians, and take on three jobs for cash.

On foot or behind the wheel, everything is simulated locally: traffic follows the roads, pedestrians wander and flee, wanted stars bring sirens and pursuit, and health, cash, missions and saves live in a canvas HUD backed by localStorage. All art is generated from code and all sound is synthesised with WebAudio; no network connection is needed after the page loads.

## Run it

Run `node serve.ts` in this directory, then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. WASD moves and drives, Shift runs, E enters or exits a car, F or click punches and shoots, mouse look orbits the camera, K saves, L loads, M mutes and Esc pauses.
